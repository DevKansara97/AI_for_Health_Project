Overview

  - 195 patients × 85 columns. There's one row per patient, and case_id equals folder.
  - Only 91 patients have a label (56 are 1 and 35 are 0, so about 62% positive). The other 104 are NaN.
    The 91 labelled patients are exactly the 91 in reasoning_audit.csv, so the label is a clinician's
    biopsy decision. The audit text shows that label=0 means two different things: either "low risk, no
    biopsy" or "cancer already confirmed, start treatment or a PSMA-PET instead". That matters for how
    you model and interpret it.
  - The label is noisy. In the audit, 18 of the 91 decisions are marked borderline and 15 uncertain. You
    could use this as a sample weight.
  - embeddings_mri.csv covers 191 of the 195 patients. Two of the four missing ones are labelled, so
    you'll need to impute or drop them if you combine the files.

  Things to be careful about

  1. bx is not the target. It maps 1:1 onto enc_type: NaN = "Follow-up – PSA + MRI", Negative = "prior
     negative biopsy", Positive = "prior PCa diagnosis". It's prior biopsy history. Keep one of the two
     as a 3-level categorical, not both.
  2. enc_type is probably your strongest context feature. About 54% of labelled patients already have a
     PCa diagnosis, and for them "no biopsy" often means "treat now". It's worth adding interaction
     features (e.g. prior PCa × PI-RADS, prior PCa × PSA).

  Step 2: Handle missing values (most missingness carries information)

  - *_flag labs (H/L or NaN): NaN means "within range", so encode as −1/0/+1. They're also redundant with
    the numeric lab value, so you could drop them.
  - smoking_pack_years (33% NaN): NaN = never-smoker, so fill with 0.
  - meds (50% NaN), pmhx (46% NaN), and medhx ("Not reported"): treat missing as none. n_comorbidities
    already summarises these. Optionally add a few binary flags (anticoagulant, 5-ARI, alpha-blocker).
    5-ARIs like finasteride lower PSA, which is clinically relevant.
  - lesion_max_mm (55% NaN): about 97 of those reports still describe a lesion size in radiology_report.
    My quick regex didn't agree with the structured value, so it needs proper extraction from the
    IMPRESSION or lesion sentence. Otherwise add a lesion_size_missing indicator.
  - lab_hba1c (77% NaN): likely only measured in diabetics. Use a missing indicator plus median
    imputation, or drop it.
  - pirads: one row is NaN ("PI-RADS NA"). Impute it or flag it.
  - lesion_zone: 7 NaN. Use an "unknown" category.

  Step 3: Encode

  - Ordinal: pirads (2–5), months.
  - One-hot: enc_type, smoking_status, lesion_zone.
  - dre: collapse to Normal / Abnormal (Abnormal + Nodus + Suspicious) / Not done. Some levels have only
    1–4 patients.
  - Binary: fh, extracapsular_ext, n_allergies.

  Step 4: Clean the numeric columns

  - Strong right skew in psa (up to 190), psad, psav, psap, psa_slope_per_step, psa_last_over_first, and
    vol. Apply log1p (psav has 6 negative values, so use a signed log or clip at 0) and then a
    RobustScaler/StandardScaler.
  - cspca has 5 rows exactly equal to 0.0. That looks like a sentinel or failed model output rather than
    a real probability. Check those rows and treat them as missing.
  - psa_trend_values (a "4.2;2.8;0.6;4.7" string): psa_first/max/slope/ratio already summarise it. You
    could add PSA doubling time or velocity (psav may already be this).

  Step 5: Free-text fields

  note_history, previous_notes, radiology_report, note_chief_complaint, and note_physical_examination are
  unique per patient. The most valuable things to pull out are:
  - Prior ISUP/Gleason grade. It's mentioned in about 28 records, and the audit shows clinicians lean on
    it heavily ("has ISUP3 already → treat").
  - Active surveillance status.
  - Lesion growth compared with the previous MRI.
  
  Either use regex rules or embed the text with a sentence encoder and reduce it with PCA. With n=91,
  avoid feeding raw TF-IDF into the model.

  Step 6: Fit the preprocessing correctly

  - Fit the imputers, scalers, and any PCA inside each CV fold (sklearn Pipeline + ColumnTransformer).
    With 91 samples, fitting on all the data leaks noticeably.
  - Use repeated stratified k-fold (e.g. 5×10) instead of a single split, and report AUC/balanced
    accuracy, since the classes are 62/38.
  - Leave the 104 unlabelled rows out of training (or use them only for semi-supervised experiments). The
    audit columns (w_*, free_text) are post-decision annotations: don't use them as input features,
    though they're useful for analysis or explanation.
